# 2611.0022 Markdown card: front matter and an outline in a sidebar

**Date:** 2026-11-21
**Status:** draft
**Author:** EP Lin
**Issue:** [#444](https://github.com/pingeplin/tarmac/issues/444) `feat(doc): front matter and an outline in a sidebar of the markdown card`
**Branch:** `feat/444-doc-sidebar`, stacked on `feat/443-doc-reading-column` (PR #449) at `0a7c896`.
**Design:** [2611.0020](../../docs/designs/2611.0020_markdown_card_reading_layout.md), slice 2 (O4, O5; design D8–D14, review decisions A1–A3). Decision ids below are this spec's own. No wire change: state is in memory only (slice 3, #445, stores it).

## Gate

The owner's decisions O4 and O5 of design 2611.0020 bind this spec:

- Front matter is parsed out of the body and shown in a sidebar as key/value rows; a list shows as chips.
- Sidebar option A: front matter on top, the outline below; the current section is marked with the agent accent; a click on an entry scrolls to its heading.
- Drag the inner edge: 160–420, standard 240. Below 160 it collapses to a 30px strip with `{}` and `≡` icons and heading ticks. A double-click on the edge sets 240.
- A wide card: open by default, and it pushes the text, which re-wraps. A 392 card: closed by default; a strip icon slides the panel over the text with no re-wrap.

Review decisions of the design (advisor, 2026-11-21) also bind it: the outline comes from marked's `heading` tokens (A1); overlay width `[min(160, w − 30), min(420, w − 30)]`; the sidebar scrolls on its own with no bar; the outline keeps element references; field values go in as text; a drag re-wraps live; a collapse keeps the width from before the drag (A3).

## Context

- **Page.** `DocTemplate.html`: `.doc-scroll` (absolute, `inset: 0`, `overflow-y: auto`, scroll bar hidden) > `.doc-prose-sizer` > `.doc-prose-scaler` (`transform: scale(zoom / K)`) > `.doc-prose#prose`. `.doc-prose` is `calc(var(--card-w) * K)` wide; `--card-w` is the card's world width, set by `tarmacDoc.layout(zoom, cardWidth, height)`.
- **Script.** `doc-render.js` defines `tarmacDoc` (`render`, `layout`, `fonts`, `theme`, `scrollTo`) and posts `docScroll`, `docLink`, `docOverLink`, `docEditing`, `docImages`. `doc-layout.js` (slice 1) defines `tarmacDocLayout.render(markdown)` and `textColumns(table)` on its own `marked.Marked` instance, DOM-free, tested in `DocLayoutScriptTests`.
- **Swift.** `DocWebView` reads the file, calls `tarmacDoc.render(markdown)` through `runInCardWorld(_:arguments:)` (`callAsyncJavaScript`, no completion handler today), and `tarmacDoc.layout(...)` from `layoutPage(viewport:)` with `CardBox.cardSize(ofBody:)`.
- **Presses.** The local mouse-down monitor (`AppController+Keys.swift`) calls `handlePress` and returns the event: a press on a doc body selects the card, then reaches the page. A press on a link does not select (`CardPress.link`, from the page's `docOverLink`).
- **Wheel.** With no card selected the board takes every wheel; over the selected card's body the wheel goes to the page.
- **Edges.** A card's resize handle zone is 6px inside its outer edge (`CardHandles.edgeThickness`); the card's scroll thumb is 10px wide, 2px from the body's right edge (`ScrollIndicator`).
- **marked tokens.** A `heading` token has `depth` (1–6), `text` and inline `tokens`; a setext heading is a `heading` token too. A heading inside a list item or a blockquote is a nested token. `walkTokens` visits tokens in document order. Raw HTML (`<h2>x</h2>`) is an `html` token.
- **Front matter today.** `---\ntitle: x\ntags: [a, b]\n---\n` renders as `<hr>` then `<h2>title: x tags: [a, b]</h2>`.
- **Theme tokens on the page** (after slice 1): `--bg1`, `--bg2`, `--line`, `--muted`, `--term-bg`, `--text`, `--prose-text`, `--agent`, `--agent-dim`, `color-scheme`. `Palette` also has `bg3` and `faint`.

## Decisions

### Front matter (`TarmacKit`, `DocFrontMatter`)

- **D1. When a block is front matter.** All of these hold: line 1 is `---` with only spaces after it; a later line is `---` or `...` with only spaces after it (the first such line closes the block); and at least one line between them is a key line. A key line starts at column 0 with a key of letters, digits, `_` or `-`, then `:`, then the end of the line or a space. Line ends are `\n` or `\r\n`; a `\r` before `\n` is not part of a line. Otherwise the markdown is unchanged and there are no fields.
- **D2. Fields.** `DocFrontMatter.split(_ markdown: String) -> (fields: [Field], body: String)`, `Field { key: String; value: Value }`, `Value = .text(String) | .list([String])`, all `Equatable` and `Sendable`. In order of the key lines:
  - `key: value` → `.text(value)`, trimmed; one pair of matching surrounding quotes (`"…"` or `'…'`) is removed.
  - `key: [a, b]` (the trimmed value starts with `[` and ends with `]`) → `.list`, split on `,`, each item trimmed and unquoted as above, empty items dropped.
  - `key:` with nothing after it, followed by one or more lines `- item` (any indent) → `.list` of the items, trimmed and unquoted. With no such lines → `.text("")`.
  - Any other non-blank line after a key line (an indented continuation, a nested key, a comment) is appended to that field: to a `.text` with one space; to a `.list` as one more item, trimmed. A line before the first key line is dropped. A blank line is dropped.
  - A key that appears twice gives two fields.
- **D3. Body.** The body is the text after the closing line's line end. `DocWebView` renders the body and passes the fields to the page.

### Sidebar rules (`TarmacKit`, `DocSidebar`)

- **D4. Numbers.** `standard = 240`, `minimum = 160`, `maximum = 420`, `strip = 30`, `grip = 8`, `textMinimum = 392` (`Placement.docSize.width`). All world px.
- **D5. Mode.** `DocSidebar.mode(cardWidth:, hasContent:, stored:, overlayOpen:) -> Mode`, `Mode = .none | .strip | .docked(width) | .overlay(width)`. `Stored { width: Double?; open: Bool? }` (nil = no choice yet).
  - `hasContent` false → `.none`.
  - The card is **wide** when `cardWidth − standard ≥ textMinimum` (632 or more).
  - Wide: `stored.open ?? true` → `.docked(clamp(stored.width ?? standard, minimum, min(maximum, cardWidth − textMinimum)))`; else `.strip`.
  - Not wide: `overlayOpen` → `.overlay(clamp(stored.width ?? standard, min(minimum, cardWidth − strip), min(maximum, cardWidth − strip)))`; else `.strip`.
- **D6. The space a mode takes.** `DocSidebar.occupied(_ mode:)`: `.none` 0, `.strip` 30, `.docked(w)` `w + grip`, `.overlay` 30. The text is laid out at `cardWidth − occupied` (the overlay lies over the text and does not change it).
- **D7. Actions.** `DocSidebar.Action = .open | .close | .resize(Double) | .reset`. `DocSidebar.apply(_:, stored:, overlayOpen:, cardWidth:) -> (stored: Stored, overlayOpen: Bool)`:
  - `.open`: wide → `stored.open = true`; not wide → `overlayOpen = true`.
  - `.close`: wide → `stored.open = false`; not wide → `overlayOpen = false`.
  - `.resize(w)`: `w < minimum` → a collapse: the stored width is kept; wide → `stored.open = false`, not wide → `overlayOpen = false`. Else `stored.width = min(w, maximum)`.
  - `.reset`: `stored.width = standard`; wide → `stored.open = true`; not wide → `overlayOpen = true`.
  - A card resized across 632 changes the mode, never `stored`. A clamp in D5 changes the width in use, never `stored.width`.
- **D8. The page's report.** The page posts `docSidebar` with `{action: "open"}`, `{action: "close"}`, `{action: "reset"}` or `{action: "resize", width: <number>}`. `DocSidebar.Action(report: Any)` accepts exactly those shapes, `width` a finite number > 0 (read through `JSONValue`, as `ScrollMetrics(report:)` does); anything else is nil, and nil changes nothing.

### Page rules (`doc-layout.js`, DOM-free, JavaScriptCore-tested)

- **D9. Outline.** `tarmacDocLayout.parse(markdown) -> {html, outline}`; `render(markdown)` stays and returns `parse(markdown).html`. `outline` is every `heading` token of depth 1–3, in document order (nested ones too), as `{depth, text}`. `text` is the heading's plain text: the text of its inline tokens with marks removed and inline code kept, whitespace collapsed and trimmed. Each such heading is rendered with the attribute `data-outline="<index>"` (its index in `outline`); no other markup changes. A raw HTML heading is not in the outline and has no attribute.
- **D10. Current section.** `tarmacDocLayout.currentSection(tops, scrollTop, viewHeight, scrollHeight) -> index`. `tops` are the headings' tops in the scroller's content, in order. When the scroller is at its end (`scrollTop + viewHeight ≥ scrollHeight − 1`) the answer is the last index. Otherwise it is the last `i` with `tops[i] ≤ scrollTop + 24`, or −1 when there is none (no entry is marked). `[]` → −1.
- **D11. Drag.** `tarmacDocLayout.dragWidth(startWidth, deltaScreen, zoom, direction, maxWidth) -> {width, collapsed}`. `width = startWidth + direction · deltaScreen / zoom` (`direction` +1 for a sidebar on the left, −1 on the right), then `collapsed = width < 160`; when not collapsed, `width = min(width, maxWidth)`. The page passes `maxWidth` from the layout it was given (D12).

### Page wiring (`doc-render.js`, `DocTemplate.html`)

- **D12. Layout call.** `tarmacDoc.sidebar(state)` with `state = {mode: "none"|"strip"|"docked"|"overlay", width, occupied, maxWidth, side: "left"}` (world px). Swift calls it after every render result and every layout, and after an action. The page sets `--text-w` to `cardWidth − occupied` and lays out the sidebar; `.doc-prose` and the wide-block cap use `--text-w` in place of `--card-w`.
- **D13. Structure.** The sidebar is a sibling of `.doc-scroll`, with its own sizer and a scaler at `scale(zoom / K)`, laid out at K, so a zoom does not re-wrap it. It scrolls on its own (`overflow-y: auto`, no visible bar). `.doc-scroll` starts `occupied · zoom` screen px from the sidebar's side. In `.overlay` the panel lies over `.doc-scroll`, with a shadow, after the 30px strip.
- **D14. Content.** A `FRONT MATTER` header and one row per field (key in `faint` mono, value as text; a `.list` as chips on `bg3`), only when there are fields. An `OUTLINE` header and one entry per outline item, indented by depth, only when there is an outline. The entry of the current section has a 2px left border in `--agent` and `--text` colour. Every key, value and entry is written with `textContent`.
- **D15. Strip.** The `{}` icon (only with fields) and the `≡` icon (only with an outline), then one tick per outline item of depth 1 or 2, the current one in `--agent`. The icons and ticks keep 6px from the card's outer edge. A click on an icon posts `open` (and scrolls the panel to its section); a click on a tick scrolls the doc to that heading.
- **D16. Grip.** In `.docked` and `.overlay`, an 8px grip on the sidebar's inner edge, `cursor: col-resize`. A press on it starts a drag (the page stops the press from starting a selection); each move calls `dragWidth` and lays the sidebar and, in `.docked`, the text out at that width (live re-wrap); a collapsed width shows the strip. The release posts `resize` with the width (a collapsed one too). A double-click posts `reset`.
- **D17. Close.** In `.docked`, a `⇤` button in the `FRONT MATTER` or `OUTLINE` header posts `close`. In `.overlay`, a click on the active strip icon or a press on the text posts `close`.
- **D18. Outline click.** A click on an entry or tick scrolls `.doc-scroll` so the heading's top is 8px below the top of the view (clamped to the scroll range).
- **D19. Render result.** `tarmacDoc.render(markdown, fields)` resolves to `{outline: n}`. Swift reads it (`callAsyncJavaScript` completion) and sets `hasContent = fields non-empty || n > 0`.
- **D20. Tokens.** `ThemeCSS.properties` adds `--bg3` and `--faint` after `--muted`.

### Swift wiring (`DocWebView`)

- **D21.** `DocWebView` holds `stored` and `overlayOpen` (in memory; a new page load keeps them), splits the front matter in its read, computes the mode with `DocSidebar` and the card width of `layoutPage`, and calls `tarmacDoc.sidebar`. A `docSidebar` message from the main frame goes through `DocSidebar.Action(report:)` and `apply`, then the page is laid out again.

## Out of scope

- Persisting `stored` (slice 3, #445). The right side and the Settings pane (slice 4, #446); `side` is always `"left"` here and `direction` +1.
- A front matter `title` shown as a heading. A full YAML parser.
- Keyboard control of the sidebar.

## Acceptance scenarios

### Front matter (`DocFrontMatterTests`)

- **S1 (basic)** [auto]: `"---\ntitle: Hello\nstatus: proposed\n---\n# Body\n"` → fields `[title: .text("Hello"), status: .text("proposed")]`, body `"# Body\n"`.
- **S2 (quotes)** [auto]: `title: "A: b"` → `.text("A: b")`; `x: 'y'` → `.text("y")`; `x: "y'` → `.text("\"y'")`.
- **S3 (inline list)** [auto]: `tags: [mcp, "api", , release-2]` → `.list(["mcp", "api", "release-2"])`.
- **S4 (block list)** [auto]: `tags:\n  - a\n  - "b"\nnext: 1` → `tags: .list(["a", "b"])`, `next: .text("1")`; `empty:` then `other: 2` → `empty: .text("")`.
- **S5 (other lines)** [auto]: `desc: one\n  two\n# note\nx: 1` → `desc: .text("one two # note")`, `x: .text("1")`; a line before the first key is dropped; a blank line is dropped.
- **S6 (no key line → unchanged)** [auto]: `"---\n\nSome text\n\n---\nmore"` → no fields, body equals the input.
- **S7 (no closing line → unchanged)** [auto]: `"---\ntitle: x\n# Heading"` → no fields, body equals the input.
- **S8 (CRLF and `...`)** [auto]: `"---\r\ntitle: x\r\n...\r\nbody"` → `title: .text("x")`, body `"body"`.
- **S9 (fence spaces)** [auto]: `"---  \ntitle: x\n---  \nbody"` → `title`, body `"body"`; `" ---\ntitle: x\n---\n"` (space before) → unchanged.
- **S10 (setext cost)** [auto]: `"---\nNote: text\n---\nbody"` → `Note: .text("text")`, body `"body"` (design D8's stated cost).
- **S11 (key shape)** [auto]: `"---\nkey:value\n---\n"` → unchanged (no space after `:`); `"---\nmy_key-2: v\n---\n"` → one field.
- **S12 (empty doc / first line only)** [auto]: `""` and `"---"` → unchanged.

### Sidebar rules (`DocSidebarTests`)

- **S13 (none)** [auto]: `hasContent` false → `.none` at any width.
- **S14 (wide threshold)** [auto]: no stored choice: card 632 → `.docked(240)`; card 631 → `.strip`.
- **S15 (docked clamp)** [auto]: card 700, stored width 400 → `.docked(308)`; card 1280, stored 400 → `.docked(400)`; stored 100 → `.docked(160)`.
- **S16 (closed wide)** [auto]: card 1280, `stored.open == false` → `.strip`.
- **S17 (overlay)** [auto]: card 392, `overlayOpen` → `.overlay(240)`; card 392 stored 420 → `.overlay(362)`; card 160 → `.overlay(130)`; `overlayOpen` false → `.strip`.
- **S18 (occupied)** [auto]: `.none` 0, `.strip` 30, `.docked(240)` 248, `.overlay(300)` 30.
- **S19 (open, close)** [auto]: wide `.open` → `stored.open == true`; narrow `.open` → `overlayOpen == true`, `stored` unchanged; the same for `.close`.
- **S20 (resize)** [auto]: `.resize(300)` → width 300; `.resize(500)` → 420; wide `.resize(150)` with stored width 260 → width 260, open false; narrow `.resize(150)` → `overlayOpen` false, stored unchanged.
- **S21 (reset)** [auto]: wide, stored `{300, false}` → `{240, true}`; narrow → width 240, `overlayOpen` true.
- **S22 (resize across the threshold keeps stored)** [auto]: stored `{300, true}`: card 1280 → `.docked(300)`; card 600 → `.strip`; card 1280 again → `.docked(300)`.
- **S23 (report)** [auto]: `{action: "open"}` → `.open`; `{action: "resize", width: 250}` → `.resize(250)`; nil for `{action: "resize"}`, `{action: "resize", width: "250"}`, `{action: "resize", width: NaN}`, `width: 0`, `width: -3`, `{action: "drop"}`, `"open"`, `[]`.

### Live (QA, `make run`)

The QA doc has YAML front matter (`title`, `status`, `date`, `tags: [a, b, c]`, a block list), h1, five h2 with h3 under two of them, a code block holding `# not a heading`, a raw `<h2>`, and enough text to scroll. A second doc has no front matter and no heading.

- **S33 (body)** [QA]: the body shows no `<hr>` and no run-together heading at its top; the sidebar shows every field; `tags` and the block list show as chips.
- **S34 (wide card)** [QA]: a 1280 card shows the sidebar docked on the left, 240 wide; the text column is centred in the rest (its centre at `248 + (1280 − 248)/2 = 764`, ±2).
- **S35 (narrow card)** [QA]: a 392 card shows the 30px strip; a click on `≡` slides the panel over the text and no line of the text moves; a press on the text closes it.
- **S36 (drag on an unselected card)** [QA]: with no card selected, a press on the grip and a drag of +60 screen px at zoom 1 selects the card and the sidebar follows to 300 during the drag; the text re-wraps; the release keeps 300. At zoom 0.5, +60 screen px gives +120.
- **S37 (collapse and reset)** [QA]: a drag below 160 shows the strip; `≡` opens it at the width before the drag; a double-click on the grip gives 240.
- **S38 (outline)** [QA]: a click on an h2 entry brings that heading to 8px below the top of the view; while scrolling, the marked entry follows; the raw `<h2>` and the `# not a heading` line are not in the outline.
- **S39 (threshold)** [QA]: a docked card resized from 1280 to 600 shows the strip; back to 1280 it is docked at the same width.
- **S40 (zoom)** [QA]: at zoom 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 the sidebar's entries break at the same words and the doc's `scroll.total / zoom` is the same within ±(1/zoom + 1).
- **S41 (no sidebar)** [QA]: the second doc shows no strip and no sidebar, and its column is where slice 1 puts it (S15 of 2611.0021: 318–962 on a 1280 card).
- **S42 (refresh)** [QA]: an edit of the doc file re-renders it; the sidebar keeps its width and mode, and the outline follows the edit.
- **S43 (others unchanged)** [QA]: text selection in the body still works; a link still opens and does not select the card; the card's left resize handle works with the strip there; the card's scroll thumb still tracks the doc.
- **S44 (sidebar scroll)** [QA]: with a long outline on a focused card, a wheel over the sidebar scrolls the sidebar and not the doc; on an unselected card it pans the board.

## Risks

- **Press routing** (S36): if a press on the grip does not reach the page on the same press that selects the card, `CardPress` gets a `.docControl` place reported by the page (as `.link` is), and a grip press then does not select the card.
- **Live re-wrap cost** (S36): a docked drag re-lays the 3× prose on every move. QA times it on a long doc; the fallback re-wraps on release with the panel over the text while dragging.
