## Gate

The owner's decision O6 binds this spec:

- A new Settings pane "Documents", after Fonts and Theme. Its icon is an SF Symbol, as on the other panes.
- Group "Markdown cards". Row "Sidebar side". Two clickable picture tiles, "Left" and "Right". The selected tile has a blue border.
- Help text: "The sidebar holds the front matter and the outline."
- Standard: Left. Stored in `app-prefs.json`.
- A change moves the sidebar of every open markdown card at once. Each card keeps its width and its open state.
- UI copy is English only.

The approved mockup is board `SettingsSide`, option 2. This spec describes the tiles from O6 only.

Design D14 also binds: on the Right, sidebar content keeps 12 px clear of the card's outer edge, where the card's scroll thumb is.

### Thumb and resize zones

- **Frame.** The card has a 1 px border (`CardBox.borderWidth`, `TarmacKit/CardBox.swift:9`) and a 30 px header (`CardBox.headerHeight`, `:8`). The body is the card less its border and header (`CardBox.cardSize(ofBody:)`), and the page fills the body: on a card 1280 wide the page spans card x 1 to 1279. "The outer edge" in this spec is the body's right edge, card x 1279.
- **Scroll thumb** (`TarmacKit/ScrollIndicator.swift:7-9`, `:38-47`): 10 world px wide, 2 world px from the **body's right edge**, whatever the sidebar does. So it spans world px 2 to 12 from the outer edge: card x 1267 to 1277 on a card 1280 wide. On the Right it lies over the sidebar.
- **Hit order** (`CardView.hitTest`, from `CardView.swift:518`; `TarmacKit/CardHit.swift:12-20`): the thumb while it is grabbable, then a resize handle, then the body.
- **Resize zones** (`TarmacKit/CardHandles.swift:9-10`, `:41-50`): 6 **screen** px edges and 20 **screen** px corners, inside the card's outer edge, measured from the card's top-left (header included). The right edge zone and the bottom-right corner are on the outer edge of a right sidebar.
- **The overlay's grip and the thumb, as built.** On the Left, a narrow card's overlay at its upper end puts the grip's right end at card x `1 + 30 + (cardWidth − 50) + 8 = cardWidth − 11`. The thumb starts at card x `cardWidth − 1 − 2 − 10 = cardWidth − 13`. So the grip is 2 px over the thumb (card 392: grip 373–381, thumb 379–389). `thumbClear` is measured from the card's edge, but the thumb's 12 is measured from the body's edge, 1 px in, and the grip needs the same 1 px on the strip's side.
- **What lies under the thumb on the Right, as built:**
  - Strip: the icons end 6 world px from the outer edge, so 6 px of each icon is under the thumb (2–12). D14's 12 px does not hold. This must change.
  - Docked or overlay panel: content starts after 12 · K padding, so 12 world px from the outer edge. That meets D14: the thumb ends at 12.
  - The docked close button `⇤` sits at the end of the first header, so 12 px from the outer edge on the Right. That meets D14. Its arrow points at the text, not at the edge it closes to.

## Decisions

### Preference (`TarmacKit`)

- **D1. Type.** `DocSidebar.Side: String, CaseIterable, Sendable { case left, right }`, in this order, with `title` `"Left"` and `"Right"`. It sits in `DocSidebar` because the sidebar's rules own it; `AppPrefs` and the page both use it.
- **D2. Value.** `AppPrefs.Values.docSidebarSide: DocSidebar.Side`, last in the initializer, `= .left`. No existing call site changes.
- **D3. Key.** `doc_sidebar_side` (design D17), snake case as the other keys. `encode` writes `,"doc_sidebar_side":"right"` **after** `inbox_preview_height`, only for `.right`. So a file with no choice keeps its bytes, and no existing bytes test changes. `testS15TheHeightIsWrittenLastAndReadsBack` still passes (it has no side); its name is kept, as `testTheThemeIsWrittenLast` kept its name when later keys came.
- **D4. Read.** `decode` reads the key as a string: `DocSidebar.Side(rawValue:) ?? .left`. So `"left"` is Left (and is not written back), and anything else is Left: another string, another type, `null`, no key, a damaged file. A bad value changes no other key, as for `theme`.

### Page state (`TarmacKit`, `DocSidebar`)

- **D5. Side in the state.** `PageState` gets `side: Side` (initializer default `.left`). `pageState(cardWidth:stored:overlayOpen:side:)` gets `side` last, default `.left`, and copies it. `arguments` sends `"side": side.rawValue` and a new key `"thumbReach": thumbReach`. `thumbReach = Double(ScrollIndicator.width + ScrollIndicator.inset)` (12) is how far the thumb reaches in from the body's edge, the page's own frame. The page then writes no clearance number of its own (2611.0022 D12's rule).
- **D6. Width and open state do not depend on the side; the grip clears the thumb.**
  - `mode`, `range`, `apply`, `storesChange`, `restored` and `Stored` take no side. `pageState` for `.right` equals the one for `.left` in every field but `side` (S6). This is what keeps each card's width and open state on a change (O6).
  - `thumbClear` becomes `thumbReach + 2 · CardBox.borderWidth + 1` = `ScrollIndicator.width + ScrollIndicator.inset + 2 · CardBox.borderWidth + 1` = 15. The range's upper end stays `cardWidth − strip − grip − thumbClear`. Then on the Left the grip ends at card x `1 + 30 + (cardWidth − 53) + 8 = cardWidth − 14`, and the thumb starts at `cardWidth − 13`: 1 px clear (*Context*, Thumb). This fixes both sides; the range has no side.
  - The new numbers: card 392: `160...339` (was 342). Card 200: `147...147` (was 150). Card 160: `107...107` (was 110). A card is resizable from 214 (was 211): 213 gives `160...160`, 214 gives `160...161`.
  - On the Right, at the upper end the grip spans card x 14 to 22. The left edge zone at zoom 0.5 is 12 world px, so the grip is clear of it at zoom 0.5 and up.
  - Accepted: at its upper end the grip's lowest 20 **screen** px are under the bottom corner zone on its side (bottom-right on the Left, bottom-left on the Right) at zoom below 20/14 (about 1.43): the zone is 20/zoom world px wide and the grip starts 14 px from the edge. On the Right, at zoom below 20/31 (about 0.65), the top-left corner zone also covers the grip's top. On the Left there is no top-right corner zone: a doc card has a close button (`CardChrome.swift:400-402`, `CardHandles.zone` `:42-44`). A press in a corner zone resizes the card.
- **D7. Changed assertions (2611.0022 tests).**
  - `test2611_0022D12ThePageState`: the arguments dictionary gains `"thumbReach": 12`. Its narrow lines change by D6: card 392 stored 420 → `strip` width 339, `maxWidth` 339; card 392 overlay → `maxWidth` 339; card 200 overlay → width 147, `maxWidth` 147. Its other lines stay, by the defaults of D5.
  - `test2611_0022S17AnOverlayKeepsClearOfTheScrollThumb` (name kept): `.overlay(342)` → `.overlay(339)`; `mode(160, overlayOpen: true)` `.overlay(110)` → `.overlay(107)`; `range(392)` `160...342` → `160...339`; `range(160)` `110...110` → `107...107`; `range(211) == 160...161` → `range(214) == 160...161`; the `resizable` pairs `(210, false), (211, true)` → `(213, false), (214, true)` (160, 392 and 1280 stay).
  - `DocLayoutScriptTests` has no range number (a grep for 342, 110, 150 and 211 finds none there): no change by D6.
  - 2611.0022 QA numbers that change (records, not re-run): spec 2611.0022 `:53` (`thumbClear = 12`; "a card wider than 210") and `:137` (S17's numbers), and `qa/doc-sidebar-qa.md` S46 "at 200 the overlay is 150" (`:55`, `:81`, `:220`), now 147. See *Docs*.

### Page rules (`doc-layout.js`, JavaScriptCore-tested)

- **D8. Side name.** `tarmacDocLayout.side(state)` is `"right"` when `state` is an object whose `side` is exactly `"right"`, else `"left"`. `direction(side)` stays as it is (+1 left, −1 right): a drag on the Right grows the sidebar to the left. The page uses `side(state)` for the attribute (D10) and for the drag.
- **D9. Panel start.** `parts` returns `panelStart` in place of `panelLeft`: the panel's start, measured from the sidebar's outer edge (`state.strip` in `overlay`, else 0). A rename only: the value does not change. `geometry`'s `offset` is measured the same way, so `geometry` needs no side. Changed assertion: the key name in `test2611_0022TheSidebarsParts`'s `box` helper.

### Page wiring (`doc-render.js`, `DocTemplate.html`)

Thin wiring and presentation (exceptions 2 and 1, discharged by QA S12–S21). The template rules have `DocTemplateTests` (S9).

- **D10. Attribute and lengths.** `applySide` sets `data-sidebar-side` to `side(state)` on `document.documentElement`, and `--side-thumb-reach` to `state.thumbReach` px, and `--side-panel-start` (renamed from `--side-panel-left`) to `parts.panelStart` px. `data-side` keeps the mode. Before the first state the attribute is absent and the left rules apply.
- **D11. Mirrored places.** Under `:root[data-sidebar-side="right"]` the template mirrors each left place of *Context*. Each mirrored rule sets only the properties named here, never a `background` shorthand: a shorthand under this selector would outrank `[data-side="overlay"] .doc-side-grip { background: none; }` and draw a hairline on a right overlay.
  - `.doc-scroll`: `left: 0; right: calc(var(--side-offset) * var(--zoom))`.
  - `.doc-side-strip`: `left: auto; right: 0`.
  - `.doc-side-panel`: `left: auto; right: calc(var(--side-panel-start) * var(--zoom))`.
  - overlay panel border: `border-right: 0; border-left: calc(1px * var(--zoom)) solid var(--line)`.
  - `.doc-side-grip`: `left: auto; right: calc((var(--side-panel-start) + var(--side-panel)) * var(--zoom)); background-position: 100% 0`, so the hairline is beside the panel.
  - The panel's content stays left to right; the entries keep their left indent and left mark.
- **D12. Clear of the thumb (design D14).** Under the same selector:
  - `.doc-strip`: `padding-right: calc(var(--side-thumb-reach) * var(--oversample-k))`. The strip stays 30 wide (a wider strip would change the text width, against D6). Its icons and ticks are centred in the inner 18 px: icons 12–30 px from the outer edge, ticks 14–28 (h1) and 16.5–25.5 (h2).
  - `.doc-side-content`: `padding-right: calc(var(--side-thumb-reach) * var(--oversample-k))`. It equals today's 12, so nothing moves; the rule ties the clearance to the thumb.
  - The thumb is not moved: it is the card's, on every card kind (2610.0003). On the Right it shows over the sidebar's outer 12 px while it shows.
- **D13. Close mark.** `.doc-side-close` on the Right has `transform: scaleX(-1)`, so `⇤` shows as `⇥`, pointing at the edge it closes to. The button keeps its place at the end of the header, 12 px from the outer edge (D12).
- **D14. A change of side.** A new state with another side changes only the attribute: no text width changes (D6), so no line re-wraps; the scroll position and the current section stay. The page needs no new entry point: `tarmacDoc.sidebar(state)` takes it, with what it does today to a kept drag width (*Context*). No drag is held at a change: the change is made in the Settings window, and the board window's loss of focus ends a held drag.

### App: setting and broadcast (`TarmacApp`)

Thin wiring (exception 2), discharged by QA.

- **D15. Owner.** A new `DocumentSettings` (`TarmacApp/DocumentSettings.swift`), in the shape of `FontSettings`: `init(prefs:)` resolves `Theme.docSidebarSide` from `prefs.values.docSidebarSide`; `var side`; `choose(_ side:)` returns for the side already chosen, else `prefs.update { $0.docSidebarSide = side }`, resolves, and calls `onChange`. `Theme` gets `static var docSidebarSide: DocSidebar.Side = .left`, "as `DocumentSettings` last resolved it", beside `fontFamilies`. The side is read at the time a state is built, so a card made after a change (a new open, a board built later, a page reload) gets it with no broadcast.
- **D16. Broadcast.** `protocol DocSidebarSideFollowing: NSView { func docSidebarSideChanged() }` and `NSView.broadcastDocSidebarSideChanged()`, in `TarmacApp/DocSidebarSideFollowing.swift`, in the shape of `ThemeFollowing` (design D19). `AppController.init` gets `documents: DocumentSettings` and sets `documents.onChange` to `tellEveryView { $0.broadcastDocSidebarSideChanged() }`, as `themeChanged` does. So every open markdown card is told, on mounted and unmounted boards, culled or not.
- **D17. Card.** `DocWebView` conforms. `sidebarState()` passes `side: Theme.docSidebarSide`. `docSidebarSideChanged()` sends `tarmacDoc.sidebar(state)` when `pageLoaded`; a page not loaded yet gets the side in `didFinish`. The one send is shared with `sidebarReported`. `stored` and `overlayOpen` are not touched: an open overlay stays open on the other side.
- **D18. Launch order.** `AppDelegate` makes `DocumentSettings(prefs:)` after `ThemeSettings` and before `RootView`, so no card exists before the side is resolved. It passes it to `AppController` and `SettingsWindowController`.
